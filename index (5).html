THE SIMPLE EXPLANATION

What exactly is MCP?

MCP stands for Model Context Protocol. A protocol is simply an agreed set of rules. MCP helps AI applications work with outside tools in a consistent way.

WITHOUT A CONNECTION

“How many webinar leads became pipeline?” Claude can explain how to measure this, but can't see your private records.

WITH AN APPROVED CONNECTION

Claude may request the relevant records through configured tools, compare them, and show a draft answer you can verify.

Important: connecting doesn't guarantee the data is complete, the account matches are right, or the conclusion is trustworthy.

Three things to remember:

An AI assistant makes the request. An MCP server offers specific capabilities. A connector is the setup that makes a particular service available to the assistant. Not all connectors expose the same actions.

THE VISUAL VERSION

Follow the information.

Think of the connector as a permission-controlled doorway. An assistant can request specific information—not everything in every app.

See the route from your question to a useful answer. The arrows show requests going out and results coming back.

MCP makes the connection more standardized; it does not magically combine every record in your technology stack. Source systems still need to support the connection, and access must be permitted.

MARKETING POSSIBILITIES

What can a marketing team do?

ACCOUNT-BASED MARKETING

Find accounts worth a closer look

Compare intent signals, account fit and campaign activity. Ask AI to explain why each account was recommended.

WEBINARS

Check follow-up gaps

Review attendees alongside approved CRM records and flag contacts or accounts needing human review.

CAMPAIGN OPERATIONS

Prepare weekly performance notes

Compare spend, responses and pipeline—making sure all numbers use the same date range.

CONTENT

Find approved material faster

Retrieve current messaging, collateral and product information from authorized content sources.

SALES ALIGNMENT

Draft an account brief

Summarize account history and supporting evidence before a seller checks and uses it.

TEAM PRODUCTIVITY

Reduce repeated lookups

Ask one question instead of visiting several systems manually, where suitable connectors are available.

These are example possibilities—not claims that every named platform currently offers each integration.

THE CMO SAFETY RULE

Easy access needs clear boundaries.

Before you connect an app

  • Check who made it. Prefer trusted providers and approved company tools.
  • Read the permissions. Can it only read—or can it edit, send, delete or export?
  • Start small. Use the smallest useful access and non-sensitive example records first.
  • Verify outputs. Ask for sources, record IDs, date ranges and definitions.
  • Require approval. Review emails, CRM updates and costly actions before execution.
  • Follow company policy. Customer information and regulated claims require extra care.
GOOD RULE TO REMEMBER

Connected AI can speed up work. A person remains accountable for the customer, the numbers, and the decision.
