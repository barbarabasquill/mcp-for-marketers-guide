READY TO START?

Connect your first app in ChatGPT.

You can start with a supported app from ChatGPT's directory. This is the easy path: no code, no terminal, no server setup.

Before you click: Use an app and account you're permitted to connect. The choices you see depend on your workspace, plan, and administrator settings. A connected app is not necessarily a custom MCP server.

FOLLOW ALONG

One small action at a time.

⚙️

Open Settings

In ChatGPT, open Settings and select Plugins. Look for the app or plugin that includes the service you want.

You should see: available apps or plugins and their connection options.

🔍

Choose an approved app

Select a familiar service. Click Install plugin if shown. Select Connect or continue setup when prompted.

You should see: a connection or sign-in screen, if that service requires it.

🔐

Check the permissions

Read what the app can access or change. Sign into the correct account. If something looks too broad, stop and ask your administrator.

You should see: the provider's approval screen or a connected status.

💬

Try a tiny, read-only question

Open a supported chat and select the connected app from the + menu or mention the app by name. Ask for one non-sensitive piece of information.

You should see: a result from the source—or a clear explanation of what is unavailable.

COPY-READY EXAMPLE

A safe first request.

Using only my approved connected app, find one non-sensitive sample document or project record I can access. Tell me its title, source, and date. Do not edit, share, send, or delete anything. If the app is unavailable, say so instead of guessing.

Compare the answer to the original source. If it cannot find anything, check the connected account, workspace permissions, and whether the app is enabled in the conversation.

Remember: Apps in ChatGPT are a broader category; not every app is an MCP connector. The beginner goal here is to learn safe connected workflows.

STAY IN CONTROL

You can change your mind.

Return to Settings → Plugins, select the connected app or account and review its available permissions, reconnection, or disconnection controls. Options differ by service.

For a business account, ask your administrator which connected services are allowed and how approvals are managed.
